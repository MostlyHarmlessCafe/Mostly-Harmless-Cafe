# Ingredients
- 340g of Greek yogurt
- 1g of dill weed
- 2.6g of salt
- 1 tbsp of extra virgin olive oil
- 1g of cayenne pepper
- 1g of ground black pepper
- 0.5g of thyme
- 2-4 cloves of garlic (grated)[^1]
- Juice and zest of &frac12; a lemon
- 1 bundle of parsley (finely-chopped)

# Instructions
1. Mix everything together in a bowl.
2. Let sit for at least 30 minutes. You let it sit for the parsley to be a bit less fibrous and for the garlic to mellow out.
3. Serve.

[^1]: I like death-by-garlic dishes as much as the next person, but it will ruin the spread if you use too much so 4 cloves is the most that I would use.
