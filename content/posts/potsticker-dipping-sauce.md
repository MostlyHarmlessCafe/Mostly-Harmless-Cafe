# Ingredients
- 50g of soy sauce
- 30g of rice vinegar
- 10g of fish sauce
- 10g of oyster sauce
- 9g of sesame oil
- 12g of chili oil
- 2 cloves of garlic (grated)
- 10g of water
- 2 green onions (finely sliced)

# Instructions
1. Mix everything into a bowl.
2. Let sit for at least 30 minutes for everything to mix together.
